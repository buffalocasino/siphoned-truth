TITLE: article: Hegseth D-Day speech attacks European allies over migration 'invasion' — June 7 2026

[NEWS NARRATIVE]
US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth used the 82nd anniversary of D-Day commemorations in France to attack European allies over migration policy, characterizing it as an 'invasion.' The Guardian described the speech as 'grotesque stupidity.' The New York Times reports Hegseth 'criticized Europe over migration invasion in D-Day speech.' French villagers in the commemoration town said Hegseth was not welcome. The Washington Post separately reports Hegseth brought six of his children to France on the official trip.
